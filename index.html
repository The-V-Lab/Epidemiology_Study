See the field as one connected system.

Epidemiology links a population question to a defensible measure, a study design, an analysis, and a carefully bounded conclusion. 流行病学把人群问题、合理指标、研究设计、统计分析和审慎结论连接成一条完整证据链。

Choose a design

Match descriptive, cross-sectional, cohort, case-control, ecological, and experimental designs to the research question.

03

Protect validity

Recognize selection bias, information bias, confounding, random error, effect modification, and limits to generalizability.

04

Interpret evidence

Read estimates, confidence intervals, screening metrics, and causal claims without overstating what the data can support.

Common research, organized by purpose.

A method becomes useful only after the research purpose is clear. These recurring question types provide a practical map of the field. 先明确研究目的，再选择方法。以下常见问题类型构成了一张实用的流行病学研究地图。

Distribution

Who, where, and when?

Descriptive studies quantify burden and reveal patterns across people, places, and time.

Determinants

What factors are associated?

Analytic studies compare groups to estimate how exposures relate to health outcomes.

Intervention

What changes outcomes?

Trials and quasi-experiments evaluate treatments, programs, policies, and preventive strategies.

Screening

How well does a test work?

Diagnostic research connects sensitivity and specificity with predictive values and prevalence.

Outbreaks

What is happening now?

Outbreak investigations combine case definitions, epidemic curves, attack rates, and rapid hypothesis testing.

Causality

What would happen otherwise?

Causal research defines a target contrast, makes assumptions explicit, and tests alternative explanations.

Learn the reasoning—not just the formula.

The V Lab tutorials treat epidemiology as disciplined reasoning under uncertainty. Code supports the argument; it does not replace design knowledge or scientific judgment. The V Lab 将流行病学视为在不确定性下进行的严谨推理：代码用于支撑论证，而不能取代研究设计知识与科学判断。

Question before method 先定义人群、时间与目标，再选择指标、设计和模型。
Estimates with uncertainty 同时报告效应大小、区间估计、假设与局限，而不只报告显著性。
Reproducibility with responsibility 让分析可检查、可复现，并把伦理、公平与隐私纳入研究全过程。
